# Buyer verification actions

Automated checks collect public website/contact evidence; they do not prove legal identity.

| Condition | Recommended next action |
|---|---|
| No URL | Find the official company website |
| Website unreachable | Confirm the company website |
| AI provider missing | Configure the AI provider and rerun review |
| AI review error | Retry the AI review |
| Low evidence score | Collect more public company evidence |
| Free email or domain mismatch | Manually check the email domain |
| No business contact | Find and confirm a business contact |
| Evidence available | Human review of public evidence |

Final identity verification remains a human task.
